1     |  INTRODUCTION


Concept drift in machine learning describes how an underlying problem's input–output linkages evolve. Any type of alteration to the data

could occur during concept drift. When the change has some degree of temporal consistency, meaning that data gathered over a while

consistently indicate the same relationship and that this relationship changes gradually over time. IoT plays an inevitable task in today's

world, and the concept drift presence in the IoT device reduces the anomaly detection models performance which causes security prob-

lems. An illustrative example of concept drift is spam detection, where the system that receives this spam represents a malicious attack

on the computer, which needs to be resolved by the detection of the concept using efficient methods. Concept drift affects the quality of

predictions and sustains high predictive performance by using concept drift detection models to detect and manage distributional

changes. Ideally, models for idea drift detection are sensitive enough to pick up drift with just a brief delay. To prevent false positives and

be trusted, concept  drift detection also  is resilient against minor input changes (Haug & Kasneci, 2021). The challenges in the

research are:

6     |  CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORKS                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    [18/10/2026].
A method for handling the concept drift issues in online data streaming is proposed in this research, which involves offline learning and incre-
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        See
mental learning. This research develops the dynamic streaming data analytic framework based on the optimized Deep CNN classifier and             the
OASW approach that effectively addresses both memory and time constraints. The concept drift in the online data streams is effectively                     Terms
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        and
predicted using the proposed approach and the proposed optimized Deep CNN classifier as a base classifier establishes the trained model for

handling the offline learning process. The effectiveness of the research relies on the proposed nature-inspired algorithm known as the Desale's                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     Conditions
aggressive hunt optimization algorithm, as it effectively trains the classifier to boost its performance. Moreover, an optimized adaptive and slid-

ing window (OASW) is utilized in this research to adapt the pattern changes in the data streams during incremental learning. The experimental

analysis with the comparative methods based on the performance metrics is found to be effective with accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, F1

score, and precision of 98.63%, 97.77%, 96.65%, 98.1487%, and 98.4469%, respectively. In future work, the ensemble classifier will be

employed to train the more complex data. The optimization techniques based on different classifiers could help further enhancement of the

results.
